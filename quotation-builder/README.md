# Quotation Builder

**Live demo:** open `index.html` (all company, product and price data in the demo is fictional).

**The problem.** Sales quotations were typed by hand in Word. Prices, stock and VAT were checked separately, so quotes were slow and easy to get wrong.

**What I built.** A browser-based quotation tool that loads the latest stock export, lets staff pick products from a searchable gallery (with sizes and stock levels), applies brand discounts and 5% VAT automatically, and prints a branded PDF quotation, pro forma invoice or delivery note.

**The result.** Replaced Word quotations for the retail and wholesale team, with consistent pricing, live stock levels and print-ready documents in minutes. Refined through 80 versions based on staff feedback.

**Built with.** HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Excel stock-export import, AI-assisted development.
